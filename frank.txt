reacher/reacher_finger_follow.py (line 1)
This is the main program. It reads the camera, tracks the finger, maps screen motion to X/Z targets, runs IK, updates the sim, and sends commands to the real robot.

reacher/joint_conventions.py (line 1)
This is the glue between sim and hardware. real_to_sim_joint_positions(...) and sim_to_real_joint_positions(...) are what make the startup pose act like “straight up.”

reacher/inverse_kinematics.py (line 1)
This is what turns a target xyz point into joint angles. If the robot misses targets or behaves strangely, this is one of the first places to inspect.

reacher/forward_kinematics.py (line 1)
This defines where the end effector should be for a given joint configuration. IK depends on this being correct.

reacher/dynamixel_interface.py (line 1)
This is the hardware I/O layer. It scans motors, reads joint positions, and sends goal positions to the real arm.

reacher/reacher_sim_utils.py (line 1)
This sets up the PyBullet visualizer and loads the sim robot. Important when the sim behavior looks wrong.
